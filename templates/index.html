01 — Signal Analysis

Listening to the shape of sound

Before any prediction, the model listens. It processes raw audio at 22,050 Hz, trims silence, and pads to a fixed 3-second window — isolating the emotional signature embedded in pitch, rhythm, and amplitude.

02 — Feature Extraction

What the CNN sees

The mel spectrogram transforms audio into a 128-band frequency image over time. The CNN branch reads this like a photograph — detecting texture, rhythm, and tonal contours that correlate with specific emotional states.

03 — Architecture

Dual-branch ensemble model

Two specialised branches process the same audio from different angles, then their outputs are fused into a single prediction.

INPUT
Raw Audio
Loaded at 22,050 Hz, silence-trimmed, padded to 3 s window.
AUGMENT
Augmentation
Time-stretch, pitch-shift, additive noise applied during training to prevent overfitting.
EXTRACT
Feature Extraction
2D mel spectrogram (128 bands) + flat 640-dim vector of MFCCs, chroma, spectral contrast, tonnetz, and pitch features.
MODEL
Dual-Branch Network
CNN processes the spectrogram image; MLP processes the flat features. Outputs fused via concatenation + dense layers.
OUTPUT
Prediction
Softmax over 4 emotion classes with confidence scores.
